AI / NLP · Full Stack

Matchwise — Explainable CV-to-Job Matching

A local-first recommendation engine that ranks candidates for a job (and jobs for a candidate) using a hybrid score: 50% sentence-embedding semantic similarity, 30% skill coverage, 20% keyword similarity. Every result ships with component scores, matched skills, missing signals and a plain-language explanation — built deliberately as decision support, not an automated hiring decision. Zero-download feature-hashing embedder by default, with optional all-MiniLM-L6-v2 via Sentence Transformers.

FastAPI · React · Sentence Transformers · Docker · pytest

E-Commerce Product Text Classification

Multi-class classification predicting product category from noisy real-world descriptions across ~50,000 records. Benchmarks three text representations head-to-head — Bag of Words (uni/bigrams), TF-IDF, and Word2Vec/GloVe embeddings — showing embedding-based models capture semantic meaning far better than frequency-based approaches.

NLP · Unsupervised Learning

News Topic Modelling & NLP Analysis

Uncovers latent themes in technology journalism using LDA and NMF side by side — a probabilistic model on a Bag-of-Words corpus versus matrix factorisation on TF-IDF. Advanced spaCy preprocessing with lemmatisation, plus targeted removal of generic journalistic filler ("said", "told") to sharpen topic separation. Surfaced clusters spanning AI innovation, regulation, industrial automation, ethics and data privacy.

spaCy · scikit-learn · LDA · NMF · WordCloud · Seaborn

MoodMix — Explainable Music Recommender

A fully local Streamlit app that builds playlists for four moods — Happy, Calm, Focused and Energetic — by ranking tracks on interpretable audio features (energy, valence, danceability, tempo, acousticness) with genre filtering, mood-match scores and a written explanation per recommendation. Runs offline with a swappable CSV catalogue, no commercial API dependency.
